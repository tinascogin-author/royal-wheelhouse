          Quick Sketch Tips

          Book Titles

          Draw an open book in a top corner to signal a title. A clapperboard can signal a movie and a speech bubble can signal a saying if those prompts come up. Break a long title into smaller clues.

          Writing & Storytelling

          Split an abstract word into parts you can draw literally. For story structure, sketch a plot mountain and point to the part you mean.

          Genres, Tropes & Themes

          Show relationships changing with faces, hearts, or two quick panels. For a genre, pair two unmistakable props.

          Art & Illustration

          Fill a simple shape with the marks used by the technique. Draw a frame first when the clue is about composition.

          Marketing

          Turn a metaphor into a literal object. For digital terms, outline a browser window and add a cursor to set the scene.

          Editing & Reviewing

          Use a page or speech bubble to show where feedback belongs. Add big proofreading marks over squiggly lines of pretend text.
